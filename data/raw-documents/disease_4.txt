    "content": "Acne lesions can be non-inflammatory or inflammatory. Open comedones (blackheads) are clogged follicles that remain open at the surface, while closed comedones (whiteheads) are clogged follicles covered by skin. Papules are small inflamed raised bumps. Pustules are inflamed lesions that contain visible pus. Nodules are larger, deeper, and often painful lesions. Cysts are deep inflammatory lesions and may increase the risk of scarring. Acne severity can be described as mild, moderate, or severe based on lesion type, extent, and the presence of deeper lesions or scarring."

    "content": "Acne susceptibility is influenced by genetics and hormonal activity. Factors that may trigger or worsen acne include hormonal changes during puberty and other hormonal states, some medicines such as corticosteroids, testosterone, or lithium, and mechanical friction or pressure from helmets, tight clothing, straps, or similar sources. Some evidence suggests that high-glycemic dietary patterns may influence acne in some people. Acne is not simply caused by poor hygiene. Stress may worsen existing acne but is not considered the sole cause."
